# Sales Performance & Customer Experience Analytics

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing sales operations and customer-related activities to evaluate business performance, understand customer behavior, and identify opportunities for improvement.

Using Microsoft Excel, the project explores sales data from multiple sources to generate actionable insights and support data-driven decision-making, primarily for the Sales Department and Customer Experience Team.

The analysis aims to evaluate sales performance, understand customer loyalty, identify operational strengths and weaknesses, and assess factors that influence customer experience.

## Business Objectives

- Evaluate overall sales performance and identify improvement opportunities.
- Analyze customer loyalty and purchasing behavior.
- Identify business strengths and weaknesses across sales operations.
- Understand customer experience through shipping and returns analysis.
- Evaluate shipping preferences and their potential impact on business operations.
- Provide actionable recommendations to support business decisions.

## Dataset Description

The project uses four datasets to analyze sales operations, shipping costs, customer behavior, and regional performance.

| Dataset | Description |
|---|---|
| Orders | Contains order details, customer information, product categories, sales, profit, discounts, and shipping information. |
| Returns | Contains information about returned orders, supporting the analysis of product returns and customer experience. |
| Shipping Cost | Contains shipping cost information for each state, enabling geographical shipping cost analysis. |
| Regional Managers | Contains regional manager information and their corresponding regions, supporting regional performance analysis and management-level insights. |

## Key Insights

### Shipping Mode Preferences

- Standard Class was the most frequently used shipping mode, accounting for **72.97% of total orders**.
- First Class was the least frequently used shipping mode.
- Standard Class represents the primary shipping preference among customers.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the shipping analysis:

- Focus promotional efforts on Standard Class due to its high usage among customers.
- Investigate opportunities to optimize Standard Class shipping costs while maintaining service quality.
- Reevaluate First Class pricing and its value proposition to understand its low adoption rate.
- Use customer purchasing and shipping patterns to identify further opportunities for improving sales operations.
